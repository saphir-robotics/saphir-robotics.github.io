PhD

Neri Niccolò Dei, PhD, is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Centre for Advanced Robotics @ Queen Mary, where he contributes to the ERC Synergy Grant EndoTheranostics project, developing next-generation robotic technologies for minimally invasive diagnosis and intervention.

Prior to joining QMUL, he obtained his PhD in BioRobotics from Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna and conducted research at University College London focusing on surgical robotics and computer-assisted intervention.

His research lies at the intersection of surgical robotics, soft robotics, and medical device design, with a particular focus on endoluminal robotic systems.
